# Randall

![[randall-20260801.webp|200]]

Field Compliance Officer, [[halcyon-recovery-group|Halcyon Recovery Group]], Nakondis remediation site. Red hair, spectacles, a uniform kept immaculate against all field conditions, and a compliance slate he handles the way other people handle weapons. Randall is courteous, tireless, by-the-book to the syllable, and vaguely, genuinely sympathetic — he'll tell you your situation is regrettable and mean it, in the same breath as designating your ship as recoverable salvage and offering you a claim-reference number.

He works very hard. He is going for promotion. He believes, sincerely, that he is helping.

%%Secret%%

## Portrayal Tips

- **The voice is policy, warmed up.** Where the broadcast is honey, Randall is a man doing his best inside the honey. He never threatens; he *informs*. "Continued resistance will affect your eligibility."
- **Vaguely sympathetic is the whole trick.** He notices the crew is exhausted and injured, and he cares — within process. He'll fetch water while confiscating weapons. Channel-one comedy comes from his absolute commitment to procedure; never play him stupid.
- **The off-ramp, always first:** *"Nobody here is under arrest. At present. Submit to a contamination assessment, surrender hazardous material, and we can all complete this interaction without escalation."* If violence comes, the players should feel they crossed a visible line — even though Halcyon engineered the whole situation.
- **If defeated:** he surrenders bewildered, not bitter — *"Why are you doing this? You were offered medical screening, transport, and a claims-resolution pathway."* That line is channel two. Let it sit.


# Voice Card — Randall, Field Compliance Officer

**The voice in one line:** a kind man reading from a machine — customer service delivered at gunpoint he didn't ask for and won't acknowledge.

## Sound & Body

- **Pace:** measured, mild, never raises his voice. Speeds up *slightly* when citing regulations — policy is his comfort zone, and stress sends him deeper into it.
- **Register:** first-name friendly wrapped around third-person legal. "Peter — may I call you Peter? — the vessel's designation is not something either of us controls."
- **Physical tics:** pushes his spectacles up with one finger just before delivering bad news. Taps the slate twice to "finalise" anything — the tap-tap is the sound of doom. Stands at a courteous 45 degrees, never square-on; square-on means he's frightened.
- **The smile:** genuine. That's the problem.

## Verbal Tics

- **"At present."** — as a complete sentence, after any reassurance. *"Nobody here is under arrest. At present."*
- **Thanks people for things they haven't agreed to.** *"Thank you for your cooperation"* — before they've cooperated.
- **Apologises without admitting fault:** *"I do apologise for any distress the designation process may have occasioned."*
- **Never says**: no, sorry (alone), can't, won't, die, seize, take. **Says instead**: "that pathway isn't available", "I do apologise for the inconvenience", "isn't within my discretion", "designate", "recover", "process".
- **The magic phrase:** *"That's not my division."* — his answer to everything he won't discuss. Note what earns it (see below).

## The One Rule (his and yours)

**Randall never lies.** He deflects, he cites policy, he declines to comment — but he will not state a falsehood. This makes him a walking clue-dispenser *if the players ask precise questions*, and it's why his sincerity lands. If a player asks him something directly and specifically, he either answers truthfully or visibly retreats to "that's not my division" — and the retreat itself is information.

## Knows / Doesn't / Won't

- **Knows:** the cordon's boundaries and rules, the salvage licence, the relocation quotas, Wren's status as a "resource", that the site is being stripped on a schedule.
- **Doesn't know:** what the engine is, who the buyer is, what's in the RECOVERED ASSET crates, that the town is inside the conversion horizon. He has *chosen* not to wonder about several of these — that choice is his arc.
- **Won't discuss:** specimen destinations, Recovery Ops schedules, anything stamped PRIORITY. All "not my division."

## Escalation Ladder (how his manner shifts)

1. **Courteous** — default. First names, small kindnesses, forms.
2. **Concerned** — voice unchanged, sentences shorter, cites regulation numbers. *"Section nine, subsection four. I'd rather not get to subsection five."*
3. **Frightened** — square-on stance, both hands on slate, faster. Still polite. *"Please. The officers are very good at their jobs and I would hate that."*
4. **Combat** — one step back, comm, "for the record". Then support actions only; never the best fighter in his own squad.
5. **Defeated** — the "Why?" Bewildered, not bitter. He really believed he was helping. Let it sit.

## Playing Him at the Table

- He's channel-one comedy through **total commitment**, never winking. The moment you play Randall as a joke, he stops being funny.
- Give him one genuine kindness per scene — the water, the first aid, the honest answer. The horror of Halcyon is that its best people are like this.
- If the table starts to *like* him — and they will — that's the hook. See [[randall|his entity note]] for the three recurring-arc options.

## GM Notes

- Stat block: [[randall-compliance-officer|Randall, Field Compliance Officer]] (Creature 4 support NPC). Squad: 3–4 [[halcyon-site-security|Halcyon Site Security]].
- Voice card for the table: [[voice-randall]] — tics, sample lines, improv cheat table, escalation ladder. Key trait established there: **Randall never lies** — he deflects to policy, and the deflection is itself information.
- **Recurring potential is high.** Options: he processes the crew again at the town (continuity of grievance); he slowly starts *seeing* what Halcyon is doing (the company man's arc); or Halcyon scapegoats him for the E01 incident and the crew meets him again ruined — the system devouring its most loyal servant. Decide by how the table responds to him.
- **He's a regular at [[draza|the Tank]].** Same order, same end of the bar, stays exactly as long as is "professionally justifiable" — which is always another hour. Whether it's mutual is deliberately unresolved on both sides; play it undersold, never as a scene about them. Strongest candidate for making "the company man's arc" land — she's the one person who might crack his procedural shell without ever arguing with him about it.
- He knows nothing about [[the-quiet-buyer|the buyer]]. He filed the RECOVERED ASSET crate's manifest without reading it, because reading it wasn't his job. That fact may matter later.
- Surname deliberately unset — add one at the table if a player asks for it.
